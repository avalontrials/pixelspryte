# How I make videos with AI

This is the plain version. No tech words.

## What this is

I make videos with AI. It is fast and it is cheap. I want to show you how.

You need three things:

1. A computer.
2. DaVinci Resolve. It is free.
3. PixelSpryte. It is free too.

## The two ways

**Way 1. AI makes the video.**

1. I ask for a video. "Make me a 30 second reel."
2. An AI writes the words.
3. AI makes the pictures, the voice, and the moving clips.
4. PixelSpryte puts it all on a timeline in DaVinci Resolve.
5. I press **Make it**. I get the video.

**Way 2. You film yourself.**

1. You film. It can be one long take.
2. You give the video to the app.
3. The app listens. It finds the quiet parts.
4. It cuts the quiet parts out.
5. It puts the good parts on a timeline in DaVinci Resolve.
6. You get the video.

The app checks the video when it is done. If it is the wrong size, or has no sound, it says so. It does not say "looks good".

Later, an AI will also read what you said and pick the best parts. That is the next step.

Both ways end in the same place: a timeline in DaVinci Resolve, and a video file.

## What makes it different

- It runs on your computer. Your video does not go to a website.
- It uses DaVinci Resolve to do the hard part.
- It checks the video when it is done. If the video is the wrong size, or has no sound, it says so. It does not say "looks good."

## How to run it

1. Open DaVinci Resolve. Leave it open.
2. Open a black window. Type this:

```
py -3.12 app.py
```

3. Open your browser. Go to `http://127.0.0.1:8765`
4. Pick a video on the left. Or make a new one.
5. Press **Make it**.
6. Wait. Watch the words in the box.
7. When it says "Done," your video is ready.

## The four shapes

| Shape | Size | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Reel | 1080 x 1920 | TikTok, Reels, Shorts |
| Portrait | 1080 x 1350 | Instagram feed |
| Square | 1080 x 1080 | Grid posts |
| YouTube | 1920 x 1080 | YouTube |

Pick one. The app makes the video the right size. You do not have to change anything in Resolve.

## What it costs

- The app: free.
- DaVinci Resolve: free.
- AI pictures and voice: about 1 to 8 dollars for one video.

That is it. No monthly fee. No seat. No cloud.
